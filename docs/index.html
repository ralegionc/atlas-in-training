Watching a Model Learn the World

15,058 places, read by two Pythia models at points through their training. A linear probe on the model's hidden states guesses where each place is. Drag the slider to watch the map form; search a place to see where the model put it at every step.

Brightness is how close the probe's out-of-fold guess lands: full at 100 km or less, dark at 3,000 km or more. Probes are ridge regressions onto a point on the globe, so a place the model knows little about is pulled to the middle of the cloud, which lies near Europe. Places from Gurnee & Tegmark (2023), names cut before any comma or bracket; Pile counts from infini-gram; coastlines from Natural Earth. Code and method: see the README.
